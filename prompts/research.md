# Research: facts, material, tones (about 10 minutes)

Collect what the builders need and nothing more. No references, no ideas, no plans for the variants: the builders
find those themselves. Never invent anything: unknowns are `[client to confirm]`.

## 1. The facts (`docs/FACTS.md`)
Open every link in the lead and search past them (Google and Google Images with the name and town, Instagram, TikTok,
Maps, marketplaces, reviews; "Sourcing" in `methods.md` has the tricks). Write, short:
- What they sell or do, where, for whom, in what languages.
- Products, services or packages with their real prices.
- Proof: real reviews, clients, numbers, awards, press, with where each came from.
- How people buy or book: WhatsApp number, phone, booking link, marketplace, address, hours.
- Brand: logo file, the brand's own colours and fonts if it has them, names or claims to avoid.
- What the user said before about this client: every message, in full and word for word (from `LEAD.md`, earlier
  rounds' notes and the thread), each with which variant it was about, and what was rejected and why. Never shorten
  or paraphrase it: a trimmed quote can turn "more professional" into "playful". Or "none".

## 2. The material (`content/`)
Download every usable photo and video (yt-dlp for reels). Prepare the best of it: crop clean, one grade, upscale only
while sharp, cut-outs where the edge is clean (`tools/cutout.py`), short clips cut from long ones (`tools/film.py`).
Then list it in `docs/FACTS.md` under **Material**: each file with its path, size, orientation (portrait or landscape)
and what it shows, and one honest line on what the material is good for and what it lacks.

## 3. The tones
Most businesses can honestly be shown in more than one tone. Work out which tones this business's customers would
believe, from what it sells, its price level, who buys and how it already presents itself. Never pick one that would
misrepresent it. Write up to five different tones, each in 3-5 words of this business's own character (not a generic
label), and give one to each of variants 1-5. If fewer tones honestly fit, variants may share a tone; the user's notes
in the lead always win. Never write "corporate", "professional" or "calm" as a tone, and add no don'ts of your own:
a corporate tone comes only from the user's words.

## 4. The seeds
Under **Seeds** in `docs/FACTS.md`, list 8-10 concrete things from the client's world that a builder could start an
idea from: its materials, tools, signature products, places, rituals, how the work gets done, what it is known for.
Things, one line each, never ideas or designs.

Add them to `docs/FACTS.md`:
```
## Tones
V1: <tone, 3-5 words>
V2: <tone, 3-5 words>
V3: <tone, 3-5 words>
V4: <tone, 3-5 words>
V5: <tone, 3-5 words>
```
