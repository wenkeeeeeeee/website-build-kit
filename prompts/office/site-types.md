# Site types and tone

## Site types
Pick one from how the business sells. It sets the pages and plumbing, not the look.
- **Shop:** many products with prices, customers who know what they want. Pages: home, catalogue with filters, product,
  about, where to buy, contact. The starter's basket ends in WhatsApp or the marketplace. If it solves a problem (skin,
  health, pain, cleaning), it sells the answer: the problem in the customer's words, how it works, real results, how to
  use it, packages. No medical promises the client can't back.
- **Branding:** premium, heritage or hard to judge in a list; the client wants a story. Home is one story moving from
  product to product; prices and the shop come after it, the main action stays in the header.
- **Booking:** people visit in person (restaurants, bars, cafés, clinics, salons, hotels). Home sells the place and the
  visit; booking or finding a branch is one tap away. Rename the basket for the business (pre-order, try-on list);
  bookings and groups go to WhatsApp.
- **Services:** expertise or case work (emcees, consultants, contractors, B2B). Home builds trust, shows the work and the
  process, then one clear enquiry. Quote form, no shop parts.

## Tone
Decide one from what the business sells and who buys, not from price. Check how the client talks in its own posts and
how its category leaders look. Mixed signals: modern and professional.
- **Playful:** joy, treats, gifts, parties, pets, casual cafés and bars, playful fashion. Loud, bold, bouncy, humour.
- **Modern and professional:** trust, performance or expertise: health, money, property, construction, education,
  technology, events, B2B. Confident, precise, clean, real proof up front.
- **Luxury:** fine dining, fragrance, jewellery, watches, boutique hotels. Rich imagery, slow pace, space, restraint.

**Character:** 3-5 words for how this business should feel, worked out fresh from how it looks and talks and what its
category leaders do. Never reused from another client. Builders decide how they show.
