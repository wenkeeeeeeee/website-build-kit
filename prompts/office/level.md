# The level

How good a hero has to be, shown by real sites the user approved as the level (2026-11-22). The category's best in
`PITCH.md` set the look and register for one client; this set says how good every hero must be, in any category.
Where it sits among the kit's files: "Which rule wins" in `AGENTS.md`.

## The bar
What the approved sites share, in every tone:
- **The product, place or person is huge and staged,** the biggest thing on screen, often set into, overlapping or
  cutting through a giant headline or wordmark. Never in a box, never one photo among many.
- **Colour is decided, not timid.** One colour or one family owns the first screen, often with a single clashing
  accent; the product and the set make the colour, not a gradient or decoration. How each variant picks its palette
  still follows "Look and category" in `taste.md` and `colour.md`.
- **One bold type move,** with very little copy: a giant word, a page-wide wordmark, a tight condensed headline.
- **Clean space:** almost no boxes, bars, badges, popups or card grids on the first screen.
- **The tone sets how loud, not how good.** Playful sites go loud (Crav, Playdate); luxury and professional ones do the
  same moves quietly (Byredo, Kononenko, Mercury, Wealthsimple). Both are the level.

## How to use it
- **Research** picks the 2–3 sites below nearest the client (same business first, otherwise the nearest way of
  selling) and captures their heroes into `PROJECT/references/level/` with `hero-frames.cjs`. They go in `PITCH.md`
  under "Level" and in every builder's brief.
- **Not references.** They show how good, never what to build: no layout, composition, colour or imagery is taken from
  them. Craft techniques still come only from references (step 3 of `research.md`).
- **Only the hero counts.** Many of these sites drop into popups or card grids below the first screen; that part is not
  the level.
- **The level review:** every hero is judged by a fresh reviewer agent beside these sites before the user sees it
  (step 4 of `build.md`).
- A site below is never also a lead reference, and anything in "Rejected" in `reference-library.md` beats this list.
