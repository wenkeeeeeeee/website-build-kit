# Rules

The few things every variant must get right. Everything else (layout, type, colour, medium, motion) comes from the
builder's references and ideas, adapted to the client.

1. **Invent nothing.** No prices, reviews, claims, names or numbers the client hasn't published. Unknowns show as
   `[client to confirm]`.
2. **It sells.** Within seconds a visitor knows what the business is (a crafted hero's object and wordmark can say it,
   with one short line: no headline block needed) and has one clear action (WhatsApp, book, buy, call) that stays in
   reach on a phone, in the header if nowhere else. Every link, number and button works.
3. **The client's real material leads.** Their own photos, videos, products, people and places, prepared properly:
   cropped clean, one grade across the site, sharp, cut out only where the edge is clean. Anything added (3D, stock,
   shaders, illustration) must look as good as a good photo, or leave it out.
4. **Works on a phone.** No sideways scroll, nothing broken, overlapping or hidden under the header. Tap targets at
   least 40px.

## Also
- **Aim:** something rarely seen, in every variant and every section. Never a usual website.
- **Motion:** creative, smooth and interactive. It runs smoothly both ways on scroll and never blocks the page. A
  corporate tone comes only from the user's own words; then it stays calm and precise, never playful, in the hero and
  below it, and is still rarely seen.
- **Heroes:** V1 and V2 each build theirs from the one reference that fits this business best (a different one each),
  adapted closely: its layout, proportions, type scale, staging and motion carry over. V3-V5 each build their own
  object or scene from the client's world; their references set only the level of finish.
- **Below the hero,** every variant's layout, motion and interaction are its own (the rest phase of `brief.md`);
  references set the level of craft and motion. Never take a reference's images, logo, words or code.
- **Colour, optional (any variant):** `prompts/office/colour.md` is a palette move (a mood pair of one colour family
  plus one strong accent from the other side of the wheel). Read it and decide for yourself: use it when the client's
  products, brand or design fit it well, and skip it otherwise. Nobody assigns it.
- **Generated media needs the user.** Don't generate images or video yourself. One crafted hero (V3) proposes 1-2
  generated objects or scenes (what it shows, where it goes, a ready prompt, a rough mock-up); every hero works
  without them. The user makes simple images in Gemini; Higgsfield
  costs credits and only runs with the user's approval. Generated media is never passed off as the client's work,
  product, people or place.
