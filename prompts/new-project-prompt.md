# New website project: the prompt

Send a client's name and links to the **Front Desk** thread. You approve the plan and the heroes, and the live link
comes back.

````markdown
Client:
Links: <website, socials, marketplace>
````

Attach the logo and photos if you have them. Send the next client straight away if you have one: up to three run at
once, and any more wait their turn.

## What happens
1. **Research, about 10 minutes.** It reads the client's links, decides the tone and a direction per variant, and picks
   references from the kit's Benchmarks and Awwwards, at least 2 per variant.
2. **Your review of the plan.** Reply "go" or send changes.
3. **Heroes first.** Each variant's hero is built and shown to you as frames beside its reference. After your "go", the
   rest of the 3 variants is built, with every page working.
4. **Quick check and publish.** It checks that nothing is broken, then goes to a private GitHub repo and a Vercel project,
   and the links come back to Front Desk for you to review.

If you paste references yourself, you get one variant per reference, and they are never replaced.

## Notes worth adding
- **Brand rules save the most rework:** a supplier name not to feature, a phrase the client hates, "don't claim halal".
- **The type of site**, if you already know it: shop, branding, booking or services.
- **The client's screen size**, if it's unusual. The default is 1920x870 at 125% zoom.

## After the build
- **Tweaks:** one line to Front Desk ("V2 hero too plain"), or a screenshot in the client's own thread. They go live again
  by themselves.
- **"Deepen V2":** when the client picks one, it fills in their full content.
- **"Wrap up <client>":** turns your tweaks into lessons, so the next job starts better.
- Full options (clone mode, capture depth, stop points): `website-build-template.md`.
